Privacy policy — draft

Website submissions are currently disabled. This proposed text is for review and does not authorize activating the form.

Identity and responsibility

WOOD U LIKE is operated under the registered business name ASSEMBLE LAB. Data controller: מתניה רוזנצויג. Privacy contact: labassemble@gmail.com.

Information in an inquiry

Inquiries may contain a name, telephone number or email address, product and quantity, dimensions, requirements and notes, engraving requests and customization ideas. Do not provide unnecessary sensitive information. Operational providers may process technical data for operation and security.

An inquiry may also include brand and site identifiers, its source page without query parameters or fragment, product identifier and name, form language, utm_source and utm_campaign labels, and the submission-attempt timestamp submitted_at. These accompany a request, separately from temporary browser storage of campaign labels. This does not confirm that a submission occurred or was received.

Purposes

Information is used to respond to inquiries, assess customization and production, prepare quotes, coordinate orders and services, and protect the service. Inquiry details are not used for advertising or marketing.

Voluntary provision

Providing information is voluntary and is not legally required to make an inquiry. Without suitable contact details we cannot respond. An inquiry is not a binding order or payment.

Service providers and international transfers

Inquiries are processed through Formspree, also used by ASSEMBL LAB, and notifications arrive in the business Gmail inbox: labassemble@gmail.com. Only the owner is authorized to log in to the Formspree account; service providers may process technical and personal data to provide the service.

Information may be processed outside Israel. International-transfer terms and applicable arrangements remain under review and must be verified before website inquiry activation is approved.

Retention and deletion

Every three months we review whether inquiries still need to be retained and delete information no longer needed, subject to legal obligations and legitimate needs. This is not a promise to delete every inquiry within 90 days.

According to the Formspree reply provided to us, information is not automatically deleted after 30 days. On the Free plan, this is a history-access limit; older information may remain with the provider even when inaccessible in the dashboard.

According to the provider reply, deletion from backups and other records requires contacting the provider. The scope, timing and treatment of copies are subject to provider processes, law and legitimate needs; there is no unconditional promise of immediate deletion or erasure of every copy. Gmail copies may also follow separate deletion processes.

Security

We apply safeguards appropriate to the risks and law. Access to the Formspree account is restricted to the owner; absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Rights and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information and correction or deletion of information that is inaccurate, incomplete, unclear or outdated. Contact: labassemble@gmail.com.

Browser storage

The website uses localStorage for language preference. Temporary campaign attribution is stored in sessionStorage and read for an inquiry; site code removes that key on entering the inquiry page, but attribution may remain in memory for inclusion with an inquiry if sending is approved. Both brands share the same GitHub Pages origin; their paths do not provide separate Web Storage isolation.

Operational providers may process technical data. The public-site network audit is not complete, so this draft does not make a final claim that cookies, tracking services or additional external services are absent.

Updates

Material updates will be published when appropriate or required by law. This draft requires owner approval of an exact revision before publication.
